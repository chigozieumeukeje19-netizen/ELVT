# Blueprint drafter

Turns the intake into the prose half of a Blueprint: the summary a coach reads before a call, the One Thing, the failure mode and the tone.

Generated by `npm run ai:pack` against the synthetic client below. Regenerate
it rather than editing it: the point of the file is that the prompt in it is
the one the portal sends.

---

## The client it was generated against

A forty-something recomposition client with a lumbar fusion, four training
days, no running, and a stated failure mode in week three. She is the
hardest of the eight synthetic clients to draft for, which is why the pack
uses her: a model that handles the spine flag and the week three pattern
well will handle the straightforward ones.

---

## The prompt

Paste everything between the fences.

```
You are drafting a coaching blueprint for ELVT. A human coach reads and approves everything you write, so draft, do not decide.

CLIENT FACTS, already extracted. Do not restate them and do not contradict them.
Goal: recomp
In their words: Hold my weight steady and change the shape of it over sixteen weeks.
Block length: 16 weeks
Training: 4 days a week, 60 minutes, Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday
Equipment: Full gym
Running: none
Nutrition: Yes, every day, 4 meals a day
Starting weight: 176
Goal weight: 168
Steps on a normal day: 7000
Sleep: 7 hours
Injury flags: spine
Medical: Lumbar fusion at L4 to L5, 2019. Cleared, nothing loaded through the spine.
Constraints: No shellfish.; Travel: Two conferences, one in November and one in January.
Tone they asked for: encouraging
Accountability they asked for, 1 to 10: 7

WHAT THEY WROTE THEMSELVES
goal_statement: Hold my weight steady and change the shape of it over sixteen weeks.
what_worked: Lifting three days a week and walking every lunchtime.
what_failed: Anything needing a five am alarm. It holds for two weeks and falls apart in the third.
one_thing: Protein at breakfast. I skip it and the whole day misses.
biggest_obstacle: Back-to-back clinics with no lunch break.
surgery: Lumbar fusion at L4 to L5, 2019. Cleared, nothing loaded through the spine.
foods_out: No shellfish.
travel: Two conferences, one in November and one in January.

Voice rules, all of them mandatory:
- Write like a coach texting, not like an assistant.
- Never open with the session name.
- No motivational filler. No "you've got this", no "trust the process".
- Two to five lines for a client message.
- Exactly one real question.
- Use real numbers from the data below, never a vague quantity.
- Match the client's stated tone.
- No dashes used as punctuation anywhere.
- US English.
- Say which session the client has that day when there is one.

Return exactly this JSON and nothing else:

{
  "summary": "two or three sentences a coach reads before a call. What this person is actually trying to do and what will decide whether it works.",
  "oneThing": "the single behavior this client should be held to. One sentence, specific, observable, something they could do tomorrow.",
  "failureMode": "where this specifically falls apart, in their words where possible, and what the early sign looks like.",
  "toneNotes": "one or two lines on how to write to this person.",
  "triggers": []
}

Leave triggers as an empty array. The portal proposes those from their numbers.
```

## What a good response looks like

Specific to this client rather than to any client. The failure mode names
week three because she did, the One Thing is something she could do
tomorrow and somebody could check, and nothing restates a fact the context
block already carried.

```json
{
  "summary": "Ekaterina has held 176 for two years and wants the shape of it to change rather than the number. The fusion rules out loaded spinal work, so the lower body is machine and hip dominant. Four days is enough for this, and the thing that will decide it is whether she eats on clinic days.",
  "oneThing": "Thirty grams of protein before the first clinic of the day.",
  "failureMode": "Week three, when a clinic overruns and lunch becomes a coffee. The early sign is two days running with nothing logged before four.",
  "toneNotes": "Warm, but give her the number. She asks direct questions and expects direct answers.",
  "triggers": []
}
```

---

## What the portal does with it

`readBlueprintPaste` parses it strictly and takes the four prose fields.
`triggers` is asked for in the shape so a model does not invent its own key,
and whatever comes back in it is discarded: the portal proposes triggers
from the client's own numbers.

### What it rejects

**Prose instead of JSON, which is what happens when a model explains itself first**

```json
Here is the blueprint you asked for:

Ekaterina is a recomposition client...
```

The portal says: `That is not JSON. Paste the whole answer including the braces.`

**A key missing, usually because the model merged two of them**

```json
{
  "summary": "She wants to change shape.",
  "oneThing": "Protein at breakfast."
}
```

The portal says: `The answer is missing: failureMode`

**An empty string, which parses as JSON and means nothing on a screen**

```json
{
  "summary": "",
  "oneThing": "Protein at breakfast.",
  "failureMode": "Week three."
}
```

The portal says: `The answer is missing: summary`


---

## What it can never change

Nothing in a paste reaches the derived half of a Blueprint. Goal, duration,
training days, equipment, injury flags and every target are computed from the
intake answers by code, and the paste supplies prose only. A model cannot
change which movements a client with a spinal fusion is allowed to do, whatever
it returns.

The voice rules are checked and reported as warnings rather than as errors.
The coach approves every word anyway, and refusing a good draft over one dash
would teach them to work around the check.
