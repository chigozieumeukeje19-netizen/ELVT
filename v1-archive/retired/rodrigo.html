The whole plan in one line

Your running does not need fixing. Your recovery and your fuelling do. Everything in here is built to move those two numbers, because they are what decide whether the fitness you already have shows up on 11 October.

Reference

Wednesday is now a full rest day

The change

You had strength on Wednesday as well as Thursday and Sunday. Wednesday is now genuinely off. No running, no lifting. Strength drops to two sessions a week, which is what you asked for in the first place.

Why this specifically

You were training seven days a week at 43, with an active calf strain, recovery scores dropping into the 20s and 30s, and real sleep debt. There was nowhere in your week for any of that to resolve.

Nothing else moved. Same mileage, same long run, same quality day. The only thing that changed is that one day a week your body gets to finish the job.

Reference

Fuelling the long run

This is the highest-value change in the whole plan

Your long runs are over two and a half hours. Above roughly 90 minutes, the research is consistent: you need carbohydrate during the run, not just before and after.

Target 60 to 90 grams of carbs per hour once you are past the first 45 minutes. That is roughly one gel every 25 to 30 minutes, or the equivalent in sports drink, chews or real food. Take the first one earlier than you think you need it.

Practise this on every single long run between now and Chicago. Race day is not the day to find out your gut does not tolerate something.

Straight after

Within 60 minutes: around 1 gram of carbohydrate per kilo of bodyweight, so roughly 80g for you, plus 25 to 30g of protein. If appetite is not there, drink it. Chocolate milk, a smoothie, a shake with fruit and honey. Liquid always beats nothing.

Why this matters more for you than most

You are on an appetite suppressant. That is fine, it is doing a job. But it means fuelling will never happen by feel, so it has to happen by plan. The number on this app is not a suggestion, it is the session.

Reference

Reading your own data

Recovery score

One low score means nothing. Three in a row, or a week averaging under 45, means the load is outrunning the recovery and we adjust. Log it every morning and the trend does the talking.

The easy run test

Several of your easy runs have been drifting into zone 4. That is not fitness, that is a fuelling and recovery signal. Easy runs should be genuinely conversational. If your heart rate climbs at the same pace week over week, you are under recovered, not unfit.

What good looks like by October

Same pace, lower heart rate. Recovery scores clustering above 50 instead of swinging. Sleep debt shrinking. Those three, in that order, are what tell us Chicago is going to go well. The mileage is already there.
