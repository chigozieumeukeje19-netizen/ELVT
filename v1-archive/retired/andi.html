Your knee

The rule

The knee routine is not a bonus. It is the thing that lets the running exist. The week you did it only a few times, your pain arrived earlier than it ever had. That is the whole argument.

Why your leg day looks like this

Glute bridges, clamshells and side lying raises are not filler. Patella tracking problems are usually a hip problem wearing a knee disguise. Strengthening the glute medius changes how your kneecap sits when you run, which is why your knee feels fine lifting and only complains on runs.

Ankle weights on the clamshells and side lying work are the progression. Same movements, more of the thing that is actually fixing it.

If it hurts

Stop that set, not the session. Then tell me. A single flagged set is information. A week of pushing through is a setback.

Sleep and the 2am wake up

What is likely happening

You train after your daughter goes down, which is the only window that works, and then you wake at 6:30. Late training keeps your core temperature and adrenaline up at exactly the hour your body wants them coming down, and a 2am waking is the classic signature of that.

We are not moving your training time. It is the thing that makes this whole program possible. Instead: magnesium glycinate before bed, box breathing at 4 in, 4 hold, 4 out, 4 hold, and lights properly down for the last thirty minutes.

Five hours at a 3 out of 10 is the lowest number in your whole check in, and it is the ceiling on your energy, your motivation and your runs. It gets attention before anything else does.
