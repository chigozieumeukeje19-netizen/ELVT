Straight talk, since you asked for it

You sat at 235 for three years. That did not happen because your training was bad, you have wrestled and you have lifted properly. It happened because nothing outside the gym was consistent.

So the two things that decide these twelve weeks are your steps and your food. Not your bench.

Reference

Why only three lifting days

This is not you doing less

You train Muay Thai on Saturday. That is a full session your body has to recover from, and it does not care that it happened in a different building.

The research on combining lifting with a combat sport lands consistently at two to three lifting sessions a week. Beyond that you are not building more, you are just eating into the recovery you need for everything else. Four lifting days plus Muay Thai is how people stall, and you have already spent three years stalled.

Why the week moved

You told me your Muay Thai gym is shut at weekends. Your whole week was built around Thai on a Saturday, so that was wrong from day one. Fixed now.

You told me you were stacking Thai onto your lifting days instead of using the Tuesday and Thursday slots, because you cannot get there on a Saturday. Fine. The plan now says what you are actually doing: Monday, Wednesday and Friday are lift plus Thai. Tuesday, Thursday and Sunday are Zone 2 walks and nothing else. Saturday is a true rest day.

Those walk days matter more now, not less. Three doubled days is a lot of load, so the easy days have to stay genuinely easy.

You asked to drop to two lifts. I have kept three, and here is the honest reason: you are eating 200 fewer calories from today, and lifting is the only thing telling your body to keep the muscle while the fat comes off. Cut a lifting day now and you lose weight that is not all fat. You said yourself three days is not too much.

Your three injuries, and how the plan handles them

Left elbow

Barbell bench is out. Every chest press in here is dumbbell, so each arm moves on its own path and the elbow is not locked into the bar. Floor press works if the bench angle bothers it.

Left big toe

Zone 2 is walking, never running. Low impact on purpose. Same aerobic work, none of the pounding.

Rib, from wrestling

Chest volume stays moderate and nothing gets pushed into an extreme stretch. If a press ever feels like it is pulling at the rib, stop that set and tell me.
