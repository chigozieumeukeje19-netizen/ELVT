How to know you're actually in it

The talk test beats the watch. If you cannot hold a full sentence without gasping, you are too fast, no matter what the screen says.

Your easy pace will feel insultingly slow for about two weeks. That is the intervention working, not a mistake. You have spent years running everything at moderate to hard, which is why your watch keeps calling you Overreaching. Easy running is the only stimulus that builds your engine without charging your recovery for it.

On hills and hot days your heart rate climbs at the same effort. Hold the heart rate, let the pace go.

Your pace target, from your own week 1 data

6:20 to 7:30 per km. Week 1 you ran 7:41 at 135 bpm. Week 5 you ran 6:24 at 134 bpm. Same heart rate, more than a minute a kilometer faster. That is the adaptation, and the old 7:00 to 7:45 band would now have you running slower than your own heart rate says is easy.

Heart rate still wins every time. If holding 6:20 pushes you over 145, slow down. The pace band tells you roughly where you should be. The heart rate tells you whether you are actually there.

Here is what to watch for: by the end of your second run you were at 125 bpm at 6:00 to 6:30 pace. Faster, at a lower heart rate. If that becomes normal rather than a one off, your engine is building and this whole band drops. That is the number I want to see move over the next month, not your training pace.
