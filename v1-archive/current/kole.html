On the days it feels like too much

Read this one on a bad day

You told me straight that depression affects your consistency. So here is the plan for those days, decided now while you are thinking clearly, rather than at 9pm when you are not.

The floor is one thing, not zero. If the full session is not happening, do the first exercise only and tick it. If the gym is not happening, walk. If nothing is happening, hit your protein and go to bed. A 20 percent day keeps the streak and the identity alive. A zero day starts a story about yourself that is much harder to stop.

Missing one day is a day. Missing three is a pattern, and that is when you message me, not after you have written the week off.
